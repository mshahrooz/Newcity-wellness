Meet the Practitioner

Mordechai Genut, MS, L.Ac.

Mordechai Genut is the owner of New City Acupuncture & Wellness Center. He is a licensed acupuncturist, holistic health practitioner, wellness coach, and martial arts expert. His practice is client-centered which focuses on providing care, compassion, attentiveness, and healing aimed at the whole person—mind, body, and spirit. He creates individualized treatment plans that target the specific needs of each person.

Mordechai’s goal is to help others maintain a mindful and well-balanced lifestyle to allow for optimal health, inner peace, and an overall good quality of life.
